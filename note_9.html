THE USER MANUAL

Subject: Player attributes

Prerequisite: Read through Book 6 before opening these notes.

Individual Interface

Every Player had a individual interface. All current known Player species use an optical interface. It is unknown if another version exists.

The individual interface is activated by the player holding their eyes steady. A blinking light appears at the edge of their vision. Using their hands, they can push this light to the center of their vision. Once in the center the light opens up into the interface.

There are many icons, which trigger functions and deeper icon menu choices. In the center of the interface, just left of center is a column of numbers. The font the individual interface appears in is tied to the Player's tier, see Inscription Fonts.

The numbers are named from top to bottom, vitality, constitution, stamina, strength, agility, dexterity, intelligence and wisdom. The maximum value of these numbers is limited by tier.

Tier 0 1 2 3 4 5 6
Max Attribute 6 36 216 1,296 7,776 46,656 279,936

Most Tier four players never reach the limit. Although Tier five and six number are given here, matching the mathematical pattern, too few players have ever reached these tiers to confirm them.

Some attributes automatically increase with tier. Intelligence values triple at each tier increase. It is believed this mechanism is put into place to enable spell learning at the new tier even after the step increase in difficulty. If a Player has a 'real' attribute value that is higher than the max, it will be masked by the max value, and the max will be used. This high 'real' value is only revealed after an increase in tier.

Player attributes

Although built into the Game interface, the names and definitions of Player attributes are a Player invention. The human version of it is based on the elven version. This section gives the quick version of their definitions.
Vitality How fast a Player recovers.
Constitution How hard it is to hurt a Player.
Stamina How long a Player can fight before exhaustion.
Strength How hard a player can hit.
Agility How fast a player can change directions.
Dexterity How flexible a player is.
Intelligence How fast a player learns.
Wisdom How much a player knows.

*Field Observation: Wisdom may be a measure of how close a player is to the next tier. For tier 0-4 wisdom has a minimum of the maximum of the tier below. This observation does not hold for tier 5 and 6. Tier 5 and 6 are well known to be test based tiers and not wisdom based. --V. K.

Nanobot Attributes

The quick version of attribute definitions are accurate, but they are not precise. Game attributes apply only to Game play. They do not apply to everyday life. That line between the two is a fuzzy one which moves at a Player increases tier. A tier zero or one player will very rarely experience nanobot assistance outside active battle, crafting or spell casting. A tier six player receives the full boost at all times even outside of the Game proper since their nanobots remain active.

Current theory that this sliding bar of assistance is the Game training Players to control those boosts, so when a Player reaches tier six they don't accidentally destroy forks everytime they eat dinner.

Below is the nanobot version of the attributes. The attributes have been reordered for clarity.

Stamina:
How much fatigue nanobots induce in the Player as payment for their use.

Vitality:
How quickly they remove the penalty fatigue induced in the Player as payment for their use. It is also how much they will assist a player to recover from natural fatigue.

Strength:
How much assistance nanobots will give to a players actions.

Constitution:
How much assistance is deducted from an attacker. At higher tiers it is also how much resistance to damage is given.

Agility:
How quickly nanobots respond to changing commands.

Dexterity:
How precise nanobots follow commands.

Intelligence:
How many repetitions are needed for nanobots to understand a new command from this Player.

Wisdom:
How well the Nanobots know the player. This is a direct measure of how integrated a Player's body nanobots are with the Player, and how well the Player is integrated with their nanobots.
