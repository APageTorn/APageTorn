THE USER MANUAL

Subject: Upgrading a Rest

Prerequisite: Read through Book 7 before opening these notes.

Rests

Rests or Rest Areas are small safe zones within the structure. They are defined by the presence of a protection crystal.

Their appearance vary across the structure depending on the decor type and tier of the rest. They all follow a standard pattern. Their blueprint is a rectangular section adjacent to a hallway with a cluster of furniture. They always include one table and two pieces of furniture that designed for sitting. They can include up to six pieces of furniture. Higher tier rests can spawn with a glass wall between the rest proper and the hallway with a door.

The protection crystal can appear anywhere in the volume of the rest including being embeded in the floor, ceiling, walls and furniture. It most commonly is found near the sofa, with above the sofa slightly more common than below it.

Current theory is the placement of the crystal may give some indication of which direction the rest will build out. As of this writing that remains unproven. --I. W.

In regions where the structure decor is ruined a quest exists to rebuild the rest. Repeated attempts at reassembling the broken frames of the furniture will grow the remaining fragments. Once the frames are complete they can be finished out to be functional chairs, tables and benches. When the furniture cluster is functionally complete, the owner of the crystal needs to either cast restore or clean the floor of the entire rest with the use of a integrated mop and mop bucket.

These actions are the first round of rest upgrading. An alternate version to regrowing the existing frames is to gather new components and replace the debris with finished furniture. Once complete the crystal will grow in size. When next the rest is empty, it will grow, doubling in size and adding a second furniture set.

Known Upgrades

Sanitation Facilities

Description:
This is a public sanitation facility that spawns off the main gallery room. It can be spawned at anytime but may not appear until later when the gallery grows long enough to reveal the door to it.

Minimum Spawn: Compost Plant seed in container
It is necessary to urinate on the seed, which is why you need a container. A seed in container spawns a sanitation facility with one compost plant and a sink. Adding a bowl of water in the original spawn will add one bathing pool filled with water.

Upgrade: Sinks/Compost plants
Small containers of water.

The smallest sized water container is taken for sinks. The number of compost plants in the finished facility will match the number of sinks. Only one compost plant seed is needed.

Upgrade: Bathing Pools
Large Bowls of Water

It is not necessary to provide bowls of the different chemicals found in the bathing pools, but to get a full set of pools one bowl per pool is required. The size of the bowl alters the size of the pool.

*Note: It is theorized here that water works because all the species involved in upgrading rests so far have used water as their primary bathing pool. Since ArTeeEe bath in dust, an experiment with an ArTeeEe on the team using dust needs to be executed. --I. W.

Kitchen

Description:
A fully built out food preparation area in a Guild Hall is the equivalent to what it available in a Square's Inn. It spawns off the main gallery on the opposite end of the center from the sanitation facility.

Minimum Spawn: Portable Stove
A single portable stove will spawn an empty room off the main gallery with the stove in it. It can be spawned at anytime but may not appear until later when the gallery grows long enough to reveal the door to it. After spawning the kitchen can be upgraded.

Upgrade: Kitchen Stove
Any addition to a bare kitchen, counter, sink, cooking ware, will cause the stove to upgrade. If those improvements are spaced over several build steps, multiple stove upgrades are possible.

Upgrade: Counters
Component built tables placed in the empty kitchen will convert to built-in counters. Shelves, pot racks, and cutting boards can also be obtained in this manner.

With the correct sizing the counters will include temperature boxes.

Upgrade: Sinks
A bowl of water left in a spawned kitchen will convert to a water source and sink. The bowl size controls sink size. Placement of the bowl roughly controls where the sink will spawn.

Upgrade: Cooking Ware
A selection of bowls, cups, spoons and knives will spawn a full set of cooking utensils. This includes the brewing tank.

Upgrade: Vendor
A selection of foods left on a counter will cause a Vendor to spawn in that location selling those items along with the standard set of food vendor items.
Example: six bottles of beer

Crafting Workshops

Description:
A workshop with tools for use in crafting. The tools are bound to the room and can not be removed. They wear out, but once broken are replaced six days later.

Minimum Spawn: Starter Tools
A full set of starter tools from one of the four base crafts will spawn a joint workshop. Workshops spawn off access hallways across from the kitchen and sanitation facility.

Upgrade: Workshop Size
Adding additional tools, both for the base crafts and more advanced crafts.

Upgrade: Tool Size
Any upgrade to a workshop will cause the included tools to upgrade. By spacing the workshop upgrades, multiple tool upgrades are possible.

Upgrade: Individual Workshops

  • 1. A full set of tier four tools will spawn a dedicated workshop for that craft.
  • 2. Crafting with each tool in the starter set causes a dedicated workshop to spawn for that craft. The tier of the spells is used to determine the size of the dedicated workshop.
  • 3. Adding a new set of tools for another craft, and crafting with them to produce a finished product, will spawn a new dedicated workshop for that craft.

Storage

Description:
A room equipped with shelving for storage. Decay rates in the room match a settled square.

Minimum Spawn: Component Shelves
A set of component shelves with items stored on them will spawn a storage room. The items stored on them can be components set for use in later upgrades.

Upgrade: Secure Storage
Component shelving matching the depth and grid of paid secure storage.

Upgrade: Armory
A Weapon stand, with weapons and armor tree with Armor on it, will spawn an Armory. This separate room spawns off the storage room and is initially locked to the crystal owner.

Barracks

Description:
This room is for overnight stays. Beds and everything owned by occupants in the room experience the decay rates of a square. All other contents decay at the wildspace rates.

Minimum Spawn: Bunk beds with leather pallets
The barracks can be spawned with a player bed of any kind. The bunk bed and leather pallet option is the must easily obtainable.

Upgrade: Size Increase
Added Bunk Beds inside the spawned barracks increases it size.

Upgrade: Private Bedrooms
Full beds from any species, set on either side of the entry doors, with an outlined component wall around them will spawn private bedrooms for the trainers use. The number of private bedrooms is limited to two.

Training Room

Description:
This room includes a sparing circle which reduces damage from spells. It provides training weapons that can not be removed from the room. They do less damage and in the case of arrows and throwing knives provide the illusion of an endless supply.

Minimum Spawn: Weapon Set
Set of weapons in a weapon stand.

1. Staff

2. Hammer

3. Ax

4. Spear

5. Bow

6. Knife

7. Sword

Upgrade: Size Increase
Adding weapon racks with weapons sets.

Upgrade: Sparing Circle Tier Increase
Use higher tier weapons for the build and upgrades.

Upgrade: Archery
Adding an archery target and demonstrating its use.

Upgrade: Virtual Opponent
Demonstrating a wide range of fighting spells.

Library

Description:
The library is the Enchantment workroom. All books bound to it become part of the Game code and can be spawned anywhere in the Game.

Minimum Spawn: Books

Upgrade: Size Increase
Adding at least six books on each turn will increase the library.

Upgrade: Shelving
Added component shelving will become integrated in the room.

Upgrade: Desks
A book binder, velum cutter and styluses will add desks to the room.

Upgrade: Catalog
On the third upgrade if desks and shelving are in place the Catalog will spawn.

Grow Room

Description:
This is the Chemistry workroom. All plants kept in the room are automatically watered. Their growth and fruit production is boosted. Fish alone with water plants can be kept in the water half of the room and are automatically fed.

Minimum Spawn: Plants in Integrated Containers
Plants in higher tier containers spawn larger versions in the grow room.

Upgrade: Increased Size
The grow room can be increases by adding plants. Water plants and fish can also be added, increasing the water half of the grow room.

Inventory Access

Description:
The Inventory Access is the standard physical interface found in the Game. It spawns in the center section of the gallery.

Minimum Spawn: Code Written on Wall.

Back door

Description:
Adds another entrance to the Guild hall that opens up on two different floors from the existing entrances.

Minimum Spawn: Escape toy.

Spell and Quest Inscription

Description:
A standard wall inscription.

Minimum Spawn: Statue

Upper Floors

Description:

Minimum Spawn: Opaque Wall
There is no known way to open the quest ability to add upper rooms to a Guild hall except to start with an Opaque hall. Once a Guild hall is built, the ability to buy the upgrade can be opened by traveling to all the upper rooms looking down onto the Guild hall and touching the glass from the back side.

Minimum Spawn: Quests
If the ability to add upper rooms is open, the repair quest for the office and one other room type has to be completed in order to open the upper rooms.

1. Bedroom, with on-suite sanitation facility.

2. Game Room.

3. Theater.

4. Office.

5. Tatoo Parlor.

5. Pet Shop.

5. Shipping Office.
