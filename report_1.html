REPORT: LANDING RETROSPECTIVE

Subject: Survey Team Seven, Day 125 After Landing
Target Reading Status: Complete after reading Book 1

SPOILER NOTICE: This is the initial report from survey team 7. Ensure you have completed Book 1 before proceeding.

Survey Team Seven, Day 125 After Landing.

A sweep of the valley to the west has discovered a ruined structure at grid 27 west 13 south. The structure is half buried in the side of the valley’s western slope. Its footprint is an octagon. The outer walls are constructed of stacked stone blocks. Three of the walls are exposed and badly damaged. There is no roof remaining, but broken columns within indicate there once was.

The two side walls that are perpendicular to the slope are more intact, and taller, but also end in broken tops. The three innermost walls appear completely intact. There are remnants above that have caught the soil of the slope that may be fragments of a missing upper floor.

The three inner walls contain openings that give access to more of the structure that is buried in the slope behind. These interior hallways show signs of damage, but are mainly intact. The construction is mostly concrete, with sections of stone tile work remaining on the floor and dark glass on the ceilings. The doorways and doors within sight are steel. The darkness within precludes any deeper exploration at this time.

It is our opinion that the front octagonal section is an entry to the structure beyond and that high water, or a landslide, has revealed it. This is a much larger fragment than what as been found previously, but matches the construction and materials reported previously. The broken columns and walls show the concrete and stone is reinforced with metal, while the remnants of the missing upper floor include I-beams and glass.

It is Survey’s recommendation that a deeper inspection of this structure is needed to determine the possible resource or danger it may present. It is too near the landing site to ignore.

There are no indications that the structure is occupied at this time.

End of Report
