[DECLASSIFIED // PRELAUNCH PLANNING // MAYFLOWER ARCHIVES]

PROJECT: TARGET SIGMA DRACONIS

Subject: Colony Basic Facts, Ship Construction, and Primary Population Metrics

1. Colony Shot Basic Facts

  • 2249 CE Agreement to build an interstellar colony ship was signed.
  • 2260 CE Primary design complete.
  • 2260 CE Construction contract won by the space yards at Titan in the Saturn system
  • 2275 CE Construction complete.
  • 2275 CE Launch with an estimated arrival time at the Sigma Draconis planetary system in 2923 CE
  • Sigma Draconis This star is located 18.8 light years from Sol.

    • A double ship design was chosen. One ship would be used for colonist transport. The second ship would be a fully automated ship that was essentially robotic equipment packed tight against a computer core and strapped onto fusion engines. These engines would not be activated until final approach at the target system. The colonist transport ship’s fusion engines provided all thrust for the launch. After obtaining flight speed, the tether was extended. The two ships spun on the 3-kilometer-long tether to provide artificial gravity for the flight crew during transport.

2. Transit & Flight Dynamics

The dual-vessel configuration completed the 648-year interstellar transit at an average velocity of 0.03c. Artificial gravity during flight was maintained via a 3-kilometer tension tether connecting the Colonist Transport and the Automated Advance Ship.

  • Tether Severance: Execute at System Margin (-180 Days to Insertion).
  • Passenger Craft: Commence a low-G, long-duration deceleration trajectory for crew comfort and system protection.
  • Automated Advance Ship: Continue on a high-velocity ballistic vector, executing a late, high-G deceleration burn to arrive 45 days ahead of the colonist transport.
  • Colony Site Selection: The Automated Advance Ship will execute scans of the planetary system on approach and make the final selection of the colonial site based entirely on pre-programmed standards.
  • Landing Surface Preparation: The Automated Advance Ship will perform a high-impact landing on unprepared ground. It will construct a landing pad for the colonist transport to execute a controlled low risk, low impact landing on. Most of the fabric of the advance ship will be consumed in the process.

3. Demographic & Biological Overview

Tests have proven natural gestation will be non-viable during the crossing due to the high levels of radiation in interstellar space. All gestation will be via artificial wombs. This restriction allows the time spread between generations to be lengthened. Maintaining a minimal overlap between generations will reduce the load on life support during the flight. A generational interval of sixty years will be targeted. This interval will be shifted slightly to produce a young adult landing generation. Young adults will be adaptable to the changed lifestyle on the planet's surface and be capable of a full day's work. Their education will be tailored for life after the landing, making them productive members from the first day.

The older members of the last generation of flight crew will provide stability and continuation of the cohort’s founding values and traditions.

Metric Specification
Active Flight Crew 10,000 (aged 83–87 years at landing)
Gestation Method Artificial Wombs (Radiation Shielded)
Landing Generation Cohort 40,000 Individuals
Age Cohort Distribution At Landing Staggered Sets: 16, 18, 20, and 22 years of age
