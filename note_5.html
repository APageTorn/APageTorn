THE USER MANUAL

Subject: Player Tier

Prerequisite: Read through Book 2 before opening these notes.

Known Player Tiers

Tier 0:

A new arrival or child. Technically and infant is tier 0, but it is generally accepted that children's attempts to learn magic below the age of eight are ignored.

A tier 0 can learn and cast tier 0 spells. They can do all utility spells, which don't require learning.

Tier 1:

Successfully cast a spell from the 1-6 spell trees.

Magic color is set. Structure born children reach tier 1 between the ages of 8-10. A tier 1 can learn and cast tier 1 spells.

Tier 2:

Cast an unknown number of tier 1 spells.

Structure born typically reach tier 2 by the age of 14. A tier 2 can learn and cast tier 2 spells.

Tier 3:

Cast an unknown number of tier 2 spells from at least 2 - 3 spell trees. Unlearned utility spells are not included in the spell tree count.

Structure born can reach tier 3 by the age of 20. A tier 3 can learn and cast tier 3 spells. As they near the tier 4 transision, tier 3 begin to leak their magic color into colored integrated items.

Tier 4:

Cast an unknown number of tier 3 spells from at least 5 of the 6 spell trees. Unlearned utility spells are not included in the spell tree count. To learn enough it is required to learn some warrior, some wizard, some crafting, some learned utility.

Structure born adults can reach tier 4 by the age of 30. Most residents never reach it. A tier 4 can learn and cast tier 4 spells. Integrated cloth discolors if the cloth is a non-matching color, or darkens if matching, if worn more than a day.

Tier 5:

Tier 5 is the first of the test tiers. A player must make the hard choice to reach tier 5.

Very few are offered the tier 5 test, so no age range can be determined. A tier 5 can learn and cast tier 5 spells. Integrated cloth discolors if the cloth is a non-matching color, or darkens if matching, within hours.

Tier 6:

Tier 6 is the second of the test tiers. A player must remain of aware of how their actions affect others even under threat of death.

Very few are offered the tier 6 test, so no age range can be determined. There are no pre-written tier 6 spells, instead a tier 6 begins to learn direct control. During this process emotions leak as they are interpreted as instructions by nearby nanobots. Nanobots' efforts to heal are no longer throttled by the control system. A tier 6 does not experience minor aches and pains, heal quickly, and grow younger. The selkie’s believe that the “upgraded spirit” of a tier 6 can bring them back to life up to six days after their death.

tier 6 can die, it just takes more effort.

A tier 6s nanobots remain active beyond the structure's area of influence.

Inside the structure a tier 6's presence affects the environment, causing changes to objects, furniture, toys and reversing some of the damage on surfaces in order to warn other players of their presence.
