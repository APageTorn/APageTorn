THE USER MANUAL

Subject: Furniture in the Structure

Prerequisite: Read through Book 2 before opening these notes.

1. The Reset

The structure is a labrynth of corridors with connecting rooms. It appears as a ruined underground building that was abandoned long ago. Most of the structure is considered wildspace as apposed to safe zones. See the section on Protection Crystals for more details on safe zones. Rooms in wildspace are decorated with a varity of items, the majority of which has the appearance of debris. When this decor or set dressing is moved within a room or removed from it, it is eventually replaced at its original position. This replacement cycle in a room is called a reset. The time between resets varies for each type of item. Not including tools and animals, a room completely resets in 6 to 36 days.

2. Room inventory types

Animals: The animals found in the rooms are always adversarial. They attack blindly and are very dangerous. When killed they drop coins and prize items. The items are copies of what can be obtained by harvesting the animal carcass. They include skins, meat, claws and tusks. Prize items are entirely virtual and go directly into inventory when touched. The organic equivalents do not go into inventory, such as the actual animal skin or meat.
Scrap: Scrap is an entirely virtual item. The visual image of it is part of the overlay. When scrap is touched it disappears and is added to inventory. Scrap can be pulled from inventory as a physical item that can then be processed into basic crafting supplies including ingots, boards and thread. Larger shop level tools will accept scrap in its digital form from a user's inventory skipping the physical form. Scrap can be metal, fiber, wood or glass.
Debris: Debris is the physical form of scrap. It can be put into inventory or fed directly into a tool to be processed into basic crafting supplies including ingots, boards and thread. Larger shop level tools will accept scrap in its digital form from a user's inventory skipping the physical form. Putting Debris into inventory reduces the amount.
Tools: Tools are items with function. They can be used in crafting or cooking. They are controlled through a tap or squeeze interface. They do not require a start or stop symbol to activate their functions. They can be put into inventory, except for very large items like anvils.
Items: These are objects that can be put into inventory, but have no controlled function. They are useful as is. Examples are plant pots, bowls and spoons.
Furniture: This is everything that will not go into inventory. This includes actual furniture, tables, chairs, beds. It also includes non-useful objects. Actual furniture comes in two types. There is component furniture and unique pieces.

*Note: Most items that go into inventory will revert to a plastic appearance when removed from the structure's area of influence. This is a sign that they are integrated. While most items that won't go into inventory remain unchanged. There are exceptions to both. --I. W.

3. Respawn Categories

A. Animals

Animal spawning is defined by region. The types of animals, number of animals per room, percentage of rooms occupied on first entry, the number of rooms that have animals added after occupation, and the respawn rates are all defined by region. Once a room that has been cleared of animals has been exited, the animals can be replaced within minutes, but it can also remain unoccupied for days.

*Note: It is my belief that animal spawning is controlled by a random number generated pick from the above described parameters. --I. W.

B. Tools

Tools appear in a spawn region. This region is a circle on the map. Within the area will be multiple rooms wth predefined locations where a tool can be placed. Tools within a spawn area will all be usable within a single craft. If a tools is collected it will be replaced by another tool within the region in thirty-six days. It can be replaced in as little as six days. Shorter replacement cycles are tied to smaller tools than the previous spawn. Example: If a loom is collected from a room, a spindle can appear within the spawn region in six days. They tool sets and the crafts they support are defined per region.

C. All Others

All other contents are replaced on a set schedule. If the room is unvisited, the items will reappear in their original location as defined in the table below. If the room is visited before the required spawn time has passed current best guess is the timer resets.

Item Type Respawn Time, (days)
Scrap 24 to 36
Debris 12 to 18
Items 6
Furniture 6
WARNING:
An complete component assembly is always Furniture, but individual disassembled items can qualify as Debris, and will respawn on the Debris schedule.

*Note: There are exceptions to these rules. When those exceptions occur it is a sign that a Narrative is active. This is especially true of odd spawns of animals. Keeping track of things that don't fit this pattern is a good way to discover new spells and quests. --I. W.

4. The Remodel

The arrangement of rooms and hallways change over time. Most areas are reworked one to two times every year. this complete rework is called a remodel. During the remodel all spawn locations and zones are redefined.
