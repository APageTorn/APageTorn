About D. R. Brown

Imagination and Adventure

I was born in Oregon and raised in Washington. I have spent most of my life in the Pacific Northwest. On my father’s side, I am the fifth generation to be born in the Pacific Northwest.

I had two careers in my working life. Straight out of high school I attended the University of Portland and obtained a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering. I worked for over ten years in the field. I completed projects in hydroelectric, pulp and paper, semiconductor and the microbeer industries. I got my second Bachelor degree in Computer Science from Portland State University. As a software engineer I have worked for employers in the aerospace, semiconductor and education industries.

I lost two years of my life to World of Warcraft. I was playing Lineage II before that. After I got over my WoW addiction I played a wide assortment of MMORPGs from Auto Assault to New World. The last one I played was Zenith: The Last City. I really, really wanted to like it because it was in VR, but it failed to catch me. I am also a veteran of No Man’s Sky, Skyrim and Minecraft. Although with Minecraft I spent most of my time playing with jigsaw structures in order to spawn a vast underground structure with a diverse set of random rooms in it. 😀

I am now retired and living outside Portland, Oregon.

All my books are entirely human-authored because I write as a hobby, and there is no joy in having a computer do it for me.

When I'm not building new worlds with narrative, I enjoy camping in eastern Oregon, exploring the native flora, learning about the history of the Pacific Northwest and observing the wildlife, (squirrels, rabbits, racoons, opossums and the rare coyote), in my yard. I also spend way too much time watching YouTube documentaries about ancient Egypt, Rome, World War II and ancient aliens.
