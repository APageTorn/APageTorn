THE USER MANUAL

Subject: Schools of Magic

Prerequisite: Read through Book 7 before opening these notes.

Definition

There are between four and seven known schools of crafting. This uncertainty arises from how humans define the schools.

Everyone agrees that Enchanting, Chemistry and Group Magic are separate magic schools.

Wizard spells, which are cast with the hands alone, could be split from Warrior skills that require a weapon. Only shield spells can be cast with and without a shield. If casting magic with an item is one type of magic, then Warrior skills and Crafter skills, which require a tool, are the same magic school. Or maybe they aren't since Warriors imbue an element into a weapon and Crafters control tools.

To make it more confusing, all those Wizard, Warrior and Crafter Hand spells could be split a different way. Hand spells are cast with three methods, finger grouping, squeezing, tapping. Each of these could be considered a magic school.

Magic that is cast with Song could be considered a separate magic, only sound can be used to cast most Wizard, Warrior and Crafting Spells, but not all.

So it could be Hand Cast, (with sound as an accessibility feature), Chemistry, Enchanting and Group Magic, or it could be Wizard, (finger grouping), Warrior, (squeezing), Crafting, (tapping), Song, Enchanting, Chemistry and Group Magic.

The Game is in many ways adaptive. Players who are very imprecise in their signaling can end up limited in what spells they can cast as Control interprets large variations down to a single symbol, leaving no room for additional symbols to be signalled. Additionally, the Game seldom limits a player to only one way to accomplish a goal. Many magic actions that can be accomplished with Hand magic can also be done with one of accepted separate schools. Usually one of these methods is stronger than the other, but they are functionally equivalent. If Hand signaling and Song were designed as separate magics but had considerable overlap between them, the Game's adaptive algorithm may have merged the two over time.

From this point of view Song is not an accessibility feature alone. Rather all the different magic schools are accessibility features that were designed to match the original species. There were eight founding species, but species 0 never played the Game. If there were 7 schools, they are all accounted for, and if there were 8, only one is missing.

NOTE :
Beyond this point we will consider the magic schools as: Wizard, (finger grouping), Warrior, (squeezing), Crafting, (tapping), Song, Enchanting, Chemistry and Group Magic.

Magic Schools

A brief description of each Magic School, along with examples.

Wizard (finger grouping)

Description: Unarmed Defense
Spells are signaled with hands alone. In this method the magic tree number are signaled by finger orientation.

Wizards can throw lightning, raise a fire wall and heal* a player.

* Wizard heal is addictive if cast between matching colors and should not be used except in cases of life and death. See the section dedicated to cast heal for further details.

Warrior (squeezes)

Description: Imbuing Elements
Spells are signaled by squeezing the handle of a weapon. The order and pattern of the squeezes with individual fingers determine the magic tree number.

Warriors imbue weapons with elemental powers, coating them in ice or fire or reducing/increasing their mass.

Crafter (taps)

Description: Creating Magical Items
Spells that control tools are signaled by tapping on the tools surface. This method is also used on built in utility items like sinks and bathing pools.

Crafters can manufacture armor, weapons and furniture.

Song

Description: Casting onto a Group
Spells are sung, with individual notes defining the magic tree numbers.

Song practitioners can conceal a group, enhance their speed or increase their recovery rate.

*Note: Because of the overlap with Wizard, Warrior and Crafter spells, this is the most difficult of the magic schools to define. To find its original use, we need to determine which function it performs better than the other three Hand casting methods. Casting spells on a group is the leading contender. This group targeting magic allows a Song practitioner to enhance all members of a group at once, since everyone that hears the sound is equally affected. They can also efficiently drive multi-passenger travel craft like boats, making Travel Magic, the next potential contender.

Enchanting

Description: Adding Magic to an Item.
Magic tree numbers are indicated with written symbols, and at tier four and above, with the color the symbol is written in.

Enchanters can reduce the weight of a bag, add swiftness to a pair of boots and add the ability to self fill to a water flask.

Chemistry

Description: Enhancing a Player
Magic tree numbers are indicated with chemicals, animal parts and plants. They are combined to make powders, creams and potions which enhance a Player when applied or drank.

Chemists produce powders, creams and elixirs. When applied or drank they can disguise a Player, allow them to breathe underwater or heal them without addiction.

Group

Description: Altering the Landscape
Cast by a group of practitioners, Magic tree number is indicated by the position of the individual who makes the indication. Known indicators that work are clapping, pounding a drum, and turning in place. The same indication must be used by all Players in the group.

A Group can raise or lower land, plant crops and cultivate them.

NOTE :
This is not to be confused with Song. Group magic is cast by a group, while Song is cast by an individual on a group.
