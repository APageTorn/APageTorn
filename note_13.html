KNOWN PLAYER SPECIES

Subject: System Index & Ethnological Profiles

Overview: The Game was originally constructed by eight founding species working in cooperation. Their native names have been lost to time; the System indexes each species strictly by numerical catalog designator.

Species 0: Unknown

CATALOG ID: 00
Status: Non-Participant
Habitat: High Radiation Environment

Species 0 never directly engaged with the Game. According to statements recorded from Tinkerer: “Their large structure and dependence on a high radiation environment for survival meant they could not interact directly with the other seven founding species.” No further biological or historical data exists in system logs.

Species 1: Unknown (The Developers)

CATALOG ID: 01
Origin: Sigma Draconis System (Gas Giant Moon)
Status: Extinct (Microbot War)

Although the biological species is presumed extinct following a destructive microbot war on their home moon, the robotic avatar for the Last Architect is explicitly modeled on their physical morphology.

Morphology:

  • Structure: Humanoid, wingless, body encased in iridescent blue feathers structurally resembling emu plumage.
  • Hands: Six-digit hands covered in bright yellow scales, terminating in short, sharp black talons.
  • Facial Features: Large red eyes; hooked beak with a black base transitioning to a white forward edge.
  • Lower Body: Three forward-facing toes per foot.

Species 16: Trolls

CATALOG ID: 16
Height Variance: 3 ft to 7 ft (Mobile) / 80 ft (Mother Tree)
Genders: 3 (Male, Female, Mother Tree)

Extremely secretive and typically traveling under disguise. Mobile Trolls physically resemble a cross between an orangutan and a wolf, featuring five-fingered ape-like hands, a wolf snout, and wolf-like feet. Their bodies are covered by gray fur that thins on their faces, hands, and feet to reveal dark brown skin.

They possess exceptionally long, bushy head hair and beards of an vibrant orangutan-red hue that completely conceal their bodies. Utilizing subtle electromagnetic radiation, Trolls can manipulate their hair to conceal and carry items. Males and females are identical in visual appearance and size variance.

The Mother Tree (Third Gender): Only one individual is known to exist. She is a sedentary, 80-foot tall sentient organism resembling a massive tree with brown bark, grey leaves, and red-orange vines. She gestates the species' young and uses her vines to capture prey to sustain herself and her offspring.

Species 23: Selkie

CATALOG ID: 23
Height / Length: 4–4.5 ft (Land) / Up to 10 ft (Stretched Swimming)
Blood Color: Blue

Resembling upright walking walruses. Though air-breathing and sleeping on land, they consider swimming a biological necessity. The vast majority of their hunting and gathering occurs in the ocean.

Morphology & Communication:

  • Features: Out of the water Selkies are short and stout with long bodies and short limbs. They have split-flipper hands (yielding two functional digits) and flipper feet. Their faces are whiskered and both genders have long tusks. Born with brown fur, their coats turn grey as they mature.
  • Sexual Dimorphism: Mature males grow much larger than females and develop a ruff or mane. They can stand 6 ft tall on land, with effort, and weigh in the tons. They find it difficult to walk upright and begin to shuffle on land using their front flippers, much like Earth walrus. In the water they can stretch out to fifteen feet. Mature Males are rare, as are mature females. It is much more difficult to detect a mature female since they only differ from younger members of the species in the amount of gray in their fur. Immature males and females are fertile, although to a lesser degree than mature individuals. The females nurse their young.
  • Language: They communicate through very high-pitched harmonic song.
  • Culture: They favor pole weapons to offset their short reach and prefer Wizard Silks for attire, as the material does not impede their swimming.

Species 34: Elves

CATALOG ID: 34
Height: 4.5 ft (Male) / 4.0 ft (Female)
Vocal Organs: None (Dental Percussion)

Elves exhibit hybrid shark and amphibian traits. They hatch from aquatic eggs as tadpoles, developing limbs before emerging onto land as air-breathing bipedal adults. They retain webbed, three-toed feet, making them exceptional swimmers despite losing underwater breathing capabilities.

Morphology & Communication:

  • Features: Four fingers per hand, large black eyes, slit nostrils, and renewable rows of sharp shark-like teeth.
  • Sexual Dimorphism: Males stand ~4.5 feet tall with sparkling, fine white scale skin. Females stand ~4.0 feet tall with deep black scaled skin.
  • Language: Lacking vocal cords, Elves communicate by grinding and clicking their teeth together. Facial expression is minimal due to tough scaled skin; emotional cues are instead communicated through subtle shifts in their hands where scale coverage is thin.

Species 36: Mer

CATALOG ID: 36
Contact Status: Unmet by Human Players
Environment: Deep Ocean Abyss

Humanity has had no direct contact with the Mer. They are an entirely aquatic species dwelling in the deep ocean trenches of Gameworld. Existing intelligence comes exclusively via Selkie accounts, who describe the Mer as "a Selkie-shaped fish."

Species 40: ArTeeEe (Harpies)

CATALOG ID: 40
Height: 3.0 ft (Adult)
Locomotion: Flight / Bipedal Reptilian

Small, feathered, flight-capable individuals strongly resembling feathered dinosaurs with reptilian facial structures and clawed feet. Their wings feature two functional fingers at the terminal joint allowing limited item manipulation, though their feet serve as primary hands during flight and resting.

Children hatch directly from eggs as fully formed miniature adults. Originally speaking a complex tonal language sounding like birdsong. They are capable of speaking the Elven dental-click dialect using their sharp, large teeth.

As of this writing there are only ten known surviving members of this species.

Species 42: Humans

CATALOG ID: 42
Status: Recent Arrivals

Humans arrived on Gameworld in a sub-lightspeed multi-generational starship. This vessel was not designed to ever fly again.

  • Features: They stand upright on two legs and are 5 - 6ft tall. They have five fingered hands. They have small dull teeth and smooth nails on their hands and feet instead of claws. Their blood is red. They have no fur, only guard hairs with the highest concentration on their heads.
  • Sexual Dimorphism: Males are larger with more muscle mass. They generally have more body hair and grow facial hair on maturity. Females are slighter but more agile. Their young are born completely helpless and are nursed by their mothers. They have wide hips and perennially enlarged adipose breasts.
  • Language: They have vocal cords, but their language is almost completely nontonal. Meaning is conveyed with syllables.
  • Culture: They are a pack society. The welfare of the average member is entirely dependent on the goals of their leadership.

*Field Note: The missing numbers are all unknown Player species that were cataloged during the long history of the Game. Whether any more of them survive in or outside the Game is unknown. --I. W.
